Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do handovers take place on campus?

Most students meet in well-lit public spots: outside the Central Library porch, ground floor of Student Activity Center (SAC), or the Omega Mess cafeteria foyer.

What happens if an item is damaged?

Both parties test and inspect the item together during handover. Any pre-existing defects should be noted. For high-value electronics, the refundable deposit covers repair costs if accidental damage occurs.

How does payment work in this prototype?

In this demo version, booking requests are simulated in browser state without real financial processing. During peer handover on campus, students use UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe) directly.
